I am a final-year Master's student in Interdisciplinary Data Science at Duke University. I study how engaging with various media platforms affects people's beliefs, behaviors, and opinions. To study this on a large scale, I apply machine learning and statistical methods to social networks, opinion surveys, and text corpora.

My current work includes [mapping podcasters'](https://sites.duke.edu/learninginnetworks/workshop-ii/) tendency to oppose socio-political institutions; uncovering how black-box models detect [anonymous online profiles](https://github.com/ananyafied/Explaining_Anonymity) with explainable / interpretable AI methods; and measuring exactly when people in my hometown, Pune, updated their beliefs about the severity of COVID-19 (and whether those beliefs reflected cognitive biases).

Before graduate school, I worked as a data analyst for two years, measuring brand reputation with social media listening and trust survey analysis at Edelman Intelligence. Before that, I completed a postgraduate diploma in economics and data analytics from the Meghnad Desai Academy of Economics, India. I hold my bachelor's degree in economics with minors in political science and sociology from Fergusson College, India.
